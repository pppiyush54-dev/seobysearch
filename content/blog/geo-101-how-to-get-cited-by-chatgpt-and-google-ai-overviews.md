If you've asked ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews a question in your industry recently, you've already seen the shift: the answer isn't a list of ten blue links anymore — it's a single, synthesized response, sometimes with a brand name or two mentioned inside it. Getting your business to be one of those names is what **Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)** is about.

## What GEO Actually Means

Traditional SEO optimises for a ranked position in search results. GEO optimises for something different: being **referenced or cited inside the AI-generated answer itself** — in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Perplexity.

The shift matters because these AI answer surfaces increasingly *are* the search experience for a growing share of queries, especially informational and comparison ones. A ranking on page one doesn't help if the searcher never scrolls past the AI summary at the top.

## How AI Models Decide What to Cite

AI models don't cite sources randomly. Across the platforms, five signals consistently show up as decisive:

1. **Entity authority** — is your brand a recognised entity in knowledge graphs and AI training data, not just a domain name?
2. **Content clarity** — does your content give clear, factual, directly extractable answers, rather than burying the point in marketing language?
3. **Source credibility** — do other authoritative sites reference and validate your claims?
4. **Structured data** — is your content machine-readable, with schema that makes claims, authorship, and organization identity explicit?
5. **Consistent presence** — is your brand represented the same way across your site, directories, social profiles, and third-party mentions?

None of these alone guarantees a citation. Together, they're what separates brands AI models trust enough to quote from brands they simply ignore.

## Where to Start

If you're beginning from zero, the highest-leverage first moves are:

- **Build a real author entity.** A generic "Team" byline gives AI models nothing to anchor authority to. A named author with a bio, credentials, and a consistent presence across your content is a trust signal AI models can actually use.
- **Structure content for extraction.** Lead with the direct answer, use clear headings, and back claims with specific data rather than generic advice — the kind of content AI models can lift a sentence from cleanly.
- **Add the right schema.** FAQPage, Article, Person, and Organization schema make the relationships between your content, its author, and your brand explicit to machines reading the page.
- **Earn third-party validation.** Citations, mentions, and backlinks from credible sources remain one of the strongest signals that a brand is safe to reference.

## How to Measure GEO Progress

Since there's no "GEO ranking report" the way there's a keyword ranking report, measurement is more manual for now:

- Run your priority topic queries directly against ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews on a regular cadence and log whether your brand appears.
- Track referral traffic from AI platforms in your analytics — Perplexity and ChatGPT increasingly send clicks, and that traffic is attributable.
- Watch competitor mentions on the same queries — if a competitor is consistently cited and you aren't, that's a content or authority gap to close.

## The Honest Caveat

No one can guarantee a specific AI citation — the models update independently and without notice, and there's no paid placement mechanism the way there is with ads. What GEO work does is build the conditions — authority, structure, clarity — that make citation more likely over time. Treat it as a compounding investment, not a switch you flip.
