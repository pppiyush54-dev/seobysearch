## **Build the trust infrastructure first, then produce sharp micro-intent pages, then earn third-party authority.**

The single highest-leverage move for seobysearch.com in 2026 is not schema or AI tricks — it is a real, credentialed author entity plus original proof (data, case studies, calculators), because Google's 182-page Quality Rater Guidelines (Sept 11, 2025 version) make Trust the foundation of E-E-A-T. Per Trustpilot's "What AI says about you" report (conducted by Seer Interactive, released May 12, 2026), businesses that actively gather and respond to reviews are cited in 

**75.3% of AI answers vs only 1%**

 for businesses with no active profile.

* **Schema is an amplifier, not a driver — structure the visible content for extraction.**

   Ahrefs' study (Louise Linehan & Xibeijia Guan, published May 13, 2026) tracked 1,885 pages adding JSON-LD vs \~4,000 matched controls and found no meaningful lift (+2.4% AI Mode, +2.2% ChatGPT, both statistically insignificant; −4.6% AI Overviews). What wins is visible structure (tables get cited \~2.5x more; listicles \~25% vs \~11% for opinion pieces) plus attribute-rich Article/Person/Organization schema. FAQ rich results were removed from Google Search on May 7, 2026 — keep FAQ content for AI extraction, not SERP dropdowns.
* **Ship a Decap CMS content model that bakes E-E-A-T and GEO into every publish**

   — an Author relation collection (bio+credentials+photo+sameAs), a Key-Takeaways/TL;DR block, a visible Q&A block, a Case Study/Proof widget, reviewed/last-updated dates, and a related-service-page linking field — each mapped to Article/Person/Organization/Service JSON-LD on the Next.js frontend, so PP publishes compliant pages without a developer.
